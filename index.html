Privacy Policy

App: OCRScan AI  |  Developer: Hussain  |  Contact: hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com

Effective: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OCRScan AI (the "Application"), operated by Hussain ("Service Provider", "we", "us"), handles information when you use the Application.

In short: OCRScan AI does not require an account and we do not operate any backend servers. The images and text you scan are processed on your device and never leave it. The only information that reaches anyone else comes from showing ads and processing purchases — collected directly by Google and RevenueCat, not by us.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or have any access to:

  • The photos or documents you scan
  • The text extracted from your scans
  • Your scan history, folders, tags, or any content you save in the Application
  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials — the Application does not have or require user accounts
  • Your contacts, location, or browsing history

All scanning, text recognition, cleanup, and translation happen using on-device processing. This data stays in the Application's private storage on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Application.

2. No backend servers

We do not operate any backend servers, and we do not directly collect, store, or process the information described in this policy on servers we own or control. Where data is collected (advertising and purchase data, described below), it is collected and processed directly by the third-party services involved — Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat — sent directly from your device to those providers, not routed through us.

3. Permissions the Application requests

  • Camera — used to scan documents. Requested only when you tap Scan, and only for the moment you're capturing a page. Photos are processed and stored locally.
  • Notifications (only if you use batch scanning of many pages in a future update) — used solely to show the progress of a long scanning batch running in the background, entirely on your device. No content is included, and nothing is sent to any external notification service.
  • Biometric / device credential (only if you enable optional App Lock in a future update) — handled entirely by your device's own authentication system; the Application never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data, or PIN.

Importing images from your gallery or files uses your device's built-in picker, which does not require granting the Application broad photo or storage access.

4. Information collected for advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays ads served by Google AdMob to support free use of the app. To do this, AdMob's SDK may collect information such as:

  • Your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and similar identifiers like the App Set ID
  • Your IP address, used to estimate a general (not precise) location
  • App interaction data, such as ad views and taps
  • Basic diagnostic data about app and SDK performance

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never receive your scan content through this process. Under our product design, ads are never shown while you're actively scanning, while a page is processing, or on the result/export screen; they appear only in less central parts of the Application. Where required by applicable law (for example, in the EEA/UK), we will request your consent before showing personalized ads.

  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps
  • Google Ads Privacy & Terms

You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID"; wording varies by Android version). Opting out reduces how tailored ads are — it does not stop ads from appearing.

5. Information collected for purchases (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat)

If you buy a subscription or make a purchase in the Application, the transaction itself is handled by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.).

We use RevenueCat, which acts as a data processor on our behalf, to manage subscription status and entitlements. Because the Application has no accounts, RevenueCat identifies your purchase using an anonymous identifier generated on your device — not any personal account of yours. RevenueCat receives purchase-related information such as the product purchased, transaction ID, purchase and renewal dates, and subscription status, used to validate purchases and prevent fraud. It never receives any scan content.

Prices are shown in the Application before you purchase and may vary by region. Subscriptions can be viewed, managed, or cancelled at any time through the Google Play Store — no sign-in to the Application itself is needed to do this.

  • Google Play Billing privacy information

6. Automatic technical data (Google Play / Android Vitals)

Like all apps on the Play Store, the Application's crash reports, "app not responding" (ANR) events, and basic performance data are automatically collected by Google Play at the operating-system level to help us find and fix bugs. We see this only in aggregate form through Google Play Console — never as content from an individual scan.

7. Optional diagnostics you choose to send

If you use the "Send Diagnostics" option in Settings, the Application prepares a report containing your device model, OS and app version, and recent error logs — never scan content. You choose where this is sent through your device's own share menu. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background.

8. What requires an internet connection

Scanning, text extraction, cleanup, saving, searching, and organizing your library all work fully offline. An internet connection is used only for: showing ads, verifying a purchase, and (in a future update) downloading an optional translation language pack. Standard data charges from your mobile provider may apply to these.

9. Where data goes

AdMob, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat are global services and may process data in countries other than your own, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses are used by these providers — see their linked policies for details.

10. Data retention

Because we have no backend server, we don't retain your data ourselves. Retention works like this:

  • Your scans, exported files, and app preferences — stay on your device only, retained until you delete them, clear them, or uninstall the Application.
  • Advertising ID and ad-related data — retained by Google AdMob per its own policy.
  • Purchase/subscription data — retained by RevenueCat and Google Play per their own policies, for as long as relevant to your subscription and for a period after for accounting/legal reasons.

11. How to delete your data

Because the Application has no accounts and no central server, there's no single database to delete from — each type of data is handled separately:

  • Scans and library content (on your device): delete individual scans anytime within the Application, or remove everything by uninstalling it.
  • Exported files (TXT, PDF, etc.): stored wherever you chose to save or share them — delete these through your device's file manager or the app you shared them to.
  • Advertising ID: held by Google, not us. Reset or opt out via your device settings (Section 4).
  • Purchase history: held by RevenueCat and Google Play as processors on our behalf. Email us at the address below and we'll submit a deletion request to RevenueCat for you, or you can contact RevenueCat or Google Play directly.

For anything not listed here, email hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com and we'll help or point you to the right provider. Requests are typically actioned within 30 days.

12. Legal disclosure

We may disclose information described in this policy if required by law (for example, to comply with a subpoena), if we believe in good faith it's necessary to protect our rights or your safety or someone else's, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a lawful government request. We do not sell your personal information.

13. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you're a California resident: some AdMob activity may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under CPRA. You can limit this using the ad-personalization controls in your device settings (Section 4). We do not sell personal information. For other CCPA/CPRA requests, contact us at the email below.

14. Children

The Application does not require a minimum age to use its core scanning features. However, because the Application shows ads via Google AdMob, we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to, or knowingly collect advertising data from, children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has been served personalized ads or had data collected in violation of this, contact us at the email below.

15. Your consent

Where we rely on your consent — for example, granting camera, notification, or biometric permission, or consenting to personalized ads where prompted — you give that consent by actively opting in to the relevant feature. You can withdraw consent at any time (for example, by revoking a permission in your device settings), without affecting anything already done before the withdrawal.

16. Security and data breaches

Because your scans never leave your device and we hold no server-side database of your data, there's very little on our end that could be breached. We take reasonable measures to keep the parts of the Application we do control (our email correspondence, our developer accounts) secure. Any breach affecting Google or RevenueCat's own systems is governed by their own breach-notification practices, linked above.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Previous versions are available on request.
