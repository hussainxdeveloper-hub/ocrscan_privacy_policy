Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OCRScan AI (the "Application"), operated by Hussain ("Service Provider", "we", "us"), handles information when you use the Application.

In short: OCRScan AI does not require an account. The images and text you scan are processed on your device and never leave it. The only information that reaches anyone else comes from showing ads and processing purchases, both handled by Google and RevenueCat as described below.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or have any access to:

  • The photos or documents you scan
  • The text extracted from your scans
  • Your scan history, folders, tags, or any content you save in the Application
  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials — the Application does not have or require user accounts
  • Your contacts, location, or browsing history

All scanning, text recognition, cleanup, and translation happen using on-device processing. This data stays in the Application's private storage on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Application.

2. Information collected for advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob may collect technical and advertising information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate location derived from your IP address, in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is governed by Google's own policies, not by us directly:

  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps
  • AdMob's data disclosure and privacy practices

You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization", wording varies by Android version).

3. Information collected for purchases (Google Play Billing & RevenueCat)

If you buy a subscription or make a purchase in the Application, the transaction itself is handled by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.).

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status and entitlements (so the Application knows what you've unlocked). RevenueCat receives purchase-related information such as the product purchased, transaction ID, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status, and device/app identifiers needed to keep this in sync. RevenueCat does not receive any scan content.

  • RevenueCat's Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing privacy information

4. Automatic technical data (Google Play / Android Vitals)

Like all apps on the Play Store, the Application's crash reports, "app not responding" (ANR) events, and basic performance data are automatically collected by Google Play at the operating-system level to help us find and fix bugs. We see this only in aggregate form through Google Play Console — never as content from an individual scan.

5. Optional diagnostics you choose to send

If you use the "Send Diagnostics" option in Settings, the Application prepares a report containing your device model, OS and app version, and recent error logs — never scan content. You choose where this is sent through your device's own share menu. Nothing is sent automatically or in the background.

6. Where data goes

AdMob, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat are global services and may process data in countries other than your own, including outside the European Economic Area. Each operates under its own safeguards and legal transfer mechanisms, which you can review in the links above.

7. Data retention

  • Your scans stay on your device only, for as long as you keep them. We cannot retrieve, back up, or delete them for you — that's entirely in your control within the Application.
  • Ad and purchase data is retained according to Google's and RevenueCat's own retention policies, linked above.

8. Your choices

  • Delete any scan at any time from within the Application.
  • Uninstalling the Application removes all locally stored scan data.
  • Adjust ad personalization through your device's advertising settings.
  • For questions about a purchase or subscription, contact us at the email below, or Google Play support for billing-specific issues.

9. Children

The Application is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.

10. Security

Because your scans never leave your device, they aren't exposed to server breaches or third-party access on our end. We take reasonable measures to keep the parts of the Application we do control (like ad and purchase integrations) secure, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Previous versions are available on request.

12. Contact us
